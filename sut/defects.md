## Notes

- D-02 and S-06 both leave products visible to the public, but the cause is different (the missing
  cascade in the model vs the missing `visible()` scope in the api controller), so they are two
  rows with two fixes.
- S-01 and S-08 show up on the same 500. S-01 is the concatenated SQL, S-08 is the trace in the
  body. Fixing one does not fix the other.
- 86 of the 352 tests fail on the delivered build. Every one of them belongs to a row above,
  nothing is left over.
